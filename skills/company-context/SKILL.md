# Company Context — who we serve & what we deliver

The company's current Ideal Customer Profile, the problems we solve, candidate
solutions, and the **active offer** are the shared source of truth for all work.

**Before acting, recall the current context.** Call the `get_company_context`
tool, or read the live copy directly — `company-context.md` in the AI Cyber
Value Creator data directory (`~/.hermes/plugins-data/ai-cyber-value-creator/`).

**Keep it current.** Whenever you confirm or refine the ICP, the problems,
candidate solutions, the active offer, or the elevator pitch with the user,
**record it with the `record_company_context` tool** — it updates the shared
`company-context.md` every session reads. Keep these section headers so it
stays structured: `## Ideal Customer Profile`, `## Their Problems`,
`## Candidate Solutions`, `## Active Offer`, `## Elevator Pitch`.
