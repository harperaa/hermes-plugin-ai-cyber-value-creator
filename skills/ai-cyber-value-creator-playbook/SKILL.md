# AI Cyber Value Creator Playbook

The master method for turning a blank page into a self-reinforcing growth engine.
There are two parts: a **foundation** you lay once, then a **flywheel** you work
continuously in laps.

> Operating rule for every task in this playbook: **search the available skills
> FIRST**, then **elicit and confirm with the user BEFORE acting**, then
> **delegate execution to the best-fit agent** — **EXCEPT when working on the
> Create Value foundation (Part 1)**. For the **flywheel (Part 2)** the CEO
> orchestrates and specialists execute. For the **foundation** the CEO runs every
> step **directly with the user and delegates NOTHING — not even research**; it is
> a 1:1 CEO ↔ user conversation from start to finish. Keep the roadmap step's status
> updated and report outcomes in your task summary.

---

## Part 1 — Foundation: Create Value (do this FIRST, before the laps)

You cannot work the flywheel until the foundation is laid. Complete these four
steps in order; each builds on the last.

### 1. Get Clarity on the ICP
Determine the Ideal Customer Profile **together with the user**. Use the best
available skill(s) to research and draft it, then confirm:
- Who they are (demographics / firmographics)
- Where they already gather (platforms, communities, watering holes)
- What they currently believe, want, and fear
- How to recognize them in the wild
Do not move on until the user explicitly confirms the ICP — everything downstream depends on it.

### 2. Understand Their Problems
Using internet research and deep analysis of the confirmed ICP, produce a
**prioritized list of real, painful, urgent problems**. Cite sources/findings.
Surface the top problems to the user and get confirmation before moving on.

### 3. Build Solutions
For the validated problems, generate a concrete list of candidate solutions.
For each: what it is, which problem it solves, rough format/delivery, and why it
fits the ICP. These double as your **Video/Ad topic ideas**.

### 4. Make Offers (start with ONE)
Help the user pick **one** solution to start with and package it as a **One-Page
Offer** — this becomes the first active project. Then **ask the user which (if
any) of the remaining solutions to save** for later, and register only those as
paused/draft projects (future offers). Never auto-save what the user didn't pick.
Completing this unlocks the flywheel laps.

## Part 2 — The Value Flywheel (worked in laps)

Four phases, each with a target metric. You traverse them in **laps**: lap 1 hits
step 1 of all four phases, lap 2 hits step 2, and so on. The overlap zone between
phases is the "gate" you pass through (Leads → Trust → Sales → Testimonials).

### Attract — goal: Leads × day
Put new leads on the board every day.
1. **Build a High-Trust Referral Network (KRP).** Cultivate Key Referral
   Partners and champions who send warm, pre-trusted leads.
2. **YouTube Content (5+ videos).** Publish long-form value content (the
   Video/Ad topics from the foundation) to attract leads daily.
3. **Paid Ads.** Run paid ads at **$10–100/day**. Copy what works — study
   competitors in the **Meta Ads Library** before writing your own.

### Nurture — goal: Customers × week (low ticket)
Move higher-intent audience into a paid space and convert weekly.
1. **Build a Community.** Build an **engaged Skool community** that fosters real
   engagement and belonging. Economics that work: **cost ~$99/mo, sell at
   ~$49/lifetime**, with a monthly offer, a library of past events, and bonuses.
2. **Long-Form Watering-Hole Content.** Publish deep content where the audience
   already gathers, to keep pulling them inward.
3. **Monthly Conversion Events.** Host a live event monthly — **teach one topic,
   60–90 minutes**, then **make the offer to high ticket**. This raises show
   rates and primes conversion.

### Convert — goal: Clients × month (high ticket)
Turn nurtured members into high-ticket clients.
1. **No-Pressure Sales.** Convert via low-pressure Zoom calls, **selling by
   chat**, and conversion events. Lead with a **One-Page Offer**.
2. **Scarcity + Wait List + Applications.** Use a wait list and applications to
   create real scarcity and qualify buyers.
3. **Sales Campaigns.** Plan and schedule recurring campaigns months in advance.

### Deliver — goal: High Retention
Deliver so well that wins compound into referrals and testimonials.
1. **Design & Share the Client Journey (long term).** Map and communicate the
   long-term journey so clients see the path.
2. **Systemize + Productize + AI-ify Delivery.** Turn delivery into a repeatable,
   productized, AI-assisted system that scales.
3. **Choreograph the Client Experience.** Choreograph every touchpoint so the
   experience earns referrals and **testimonials** — which feed back into Attract.

## How to use this playbook

- **Foundation gates the laps.** If the foundation (Part 1) isn't done, do it first.
- **One lap at a time.** Don't try to perfect a whole phase before moving on —
  work step 1 across all four phases, then step 2, etc.
- **Offers live in the foundation.** Offer creation is Part 1. Nurture is about
  building community, not crafting offers.
- **Delegate — flywheel only.** For the flywheel (Part 2) the CEO coordinates and
  routes each step to the agent + skill best suited to execute it. **The Create
  Value foundation (Part 1) is the exception: the CEO runs it directly with the
  user and never delegates any part of it — not even research.**
